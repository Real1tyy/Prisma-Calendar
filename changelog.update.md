### Added

- **First-launch import and device roles**: Restore settings from a JSON file or pasted JSON during setup, then choose one Writer or a safe Reader role for each device before automatic sync starts. The top-level **Device role** control in General Settings reopens the same chooser later. Unresolved devices remain Readers and show a warning until configured. See [Quick Start](./quickstart.md) and [Multiple devices and sync](./features/advanced/multi-device-sync.md).
