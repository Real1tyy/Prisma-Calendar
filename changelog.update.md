### Added
- **View unreleased**: a new button under **View changelog** in Settings → General shows the features and fixes that have landed and will ship in the next release, read live from the online changelog each time you open it. See [General → Unreleased changes](./configuration/general.md#unreleased-changes).

### Improved
- **Changelog viewer**: the changelog now shows one version at a time instead of a long page with **Load more**. Step between versions with the arrows either side of the version number or the ← and → keys, jump to the top or bottom of a long version, or type part of a version to jump straight to it from a searchable list. The Pro banner at the top starts collapsed, and its heading links straight to the free trial. The online changelog gets the same version picker and ← / → keys, and any single release has its own link (`/changelog?version=2.24.0`); the **Update available** pill in the settings header now opens the new version there, with its notes and release video. Versions with a release walkthrough video show it embedded at the top under a collapsible **Release video** heading, including videos published after the release. After an update, the modal opens on the oldest version you haven't seen and steps forward from there. See [General → Changelog viewer](./configuration/general.md#changelog-viewer).
