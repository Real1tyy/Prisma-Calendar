### Added

- **Note content in event modals**: create and edit event modals include a full-width plain-text **Note content** field, so agendas, meeting notes, and links can be saved without opening the note separately. The modal grows with its form until the viewport limit, with one outer scroll area, and the sticky event header plus up/down arrow controls keep long forms navigable. The field follows external note edits while it is untouched, warns before overwriting a concurrent edit, participates in undo, and is reachable with the header button or `Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+N`.
