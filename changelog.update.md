### Added

- Report a bug with pictures of it: new **Report a bug** and **Take a screenshot & report a bug** commands (both hotkey-bindable) sit beside **Send feedback**, and the report itself now minimizes — press 📷 to put it away behind a capture bar, go to the broken view, hit Enter to photograph the Obsidian window, and the report returns with the picture attached and everything you typed intact. Repeat for up to three pictures; ⤓ minimizes without capturing, with commands to restore the report or shoot straight into it.

### Improved

- **Send feedback** opens on general feedback wherever you start it — the button under General and the command now agree, with **Report a bug** as the separate entry that ticks debug info for you.
