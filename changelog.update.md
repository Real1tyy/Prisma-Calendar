### Fixed

- **New events appear immediately, even when Obsidian is busy**: an event you created, duplicated, imported, moved to another planning system, restored with undo, or that a recurring event generated could stay off the calendar for minutes — until a manual refresh — while another plugin or a sync tool kept Obsidian's indexer busy. Pressing **Refresh** repeatedly no longer stacks full rescans that could freeze the calendar; a refresh while one is running joins it.
