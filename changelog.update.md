### Added

- New **Doctor** page — open it from the command palette or the General settings page to see whether indexing, settings and your license are healthy, each with a plain-language explanation and a one-click fix where one exists. **Copy debug** puts a ready-to-paste bundle of the last 15 minutes of activity, every recent warning and error, and your version and platform on the clipboard (or saves it to a file) for a bug report — folder and note names are abbreviated and secrets are never included.
