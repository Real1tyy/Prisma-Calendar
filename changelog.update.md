### Added

- **Collapsible calendar header on mobile**: on a phone — or any pane under 768px — the calendar opens with its header collapsed to a single chevron, so the grid gets the whole screen instead of five rows of chrome. Tapping the chevron opens exactly two rows: navigation, **Create**, **Filters** and **Tabs** on the first; the untracked-events menu, **Events**, the date, **Batch**, the all-day control and **Views** on the second. **Tabs** and **Views** each reveal one more row (the view tabs and the **year / month / week / day / list** switcher) and both start hidden, so anything you don't need costs no space. **Batch Select** reads **Batch** at this width. Nothing changes on desktop. See [Calendar View → Mobile layout](./features/calendar/calendar-view.md#mobile-layout).
- **All-day display modes**: a new **All-day** button in week and day views cycles the all-day strip through three states — the usual capped strip with its **+N more** link, a full-height strip showing every all-day event with nothing hidden or clipped, and no strip at all so the timed grid takes the whole height. The label tells you which one you're in, and the choice lasts until you close the view. Month, year, and list views keep their own **+N more** behavior. Works on mobile and desktop. See [Calendar View → All-Day Display Modes](./features/calendar/calendar-view.md#all-day-display-modes).

### Fixed

- **Mobile events per day: 0 now means unlimited**: setting **Mobile events per day** to 0 did not lift the cap on phones, even though the setting says 0 is unlimited. It now behaves like **Desktop events per day**. See [Calendar UI](./configuration/calendar-ui.md).
