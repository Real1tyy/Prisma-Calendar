### Added

- **Multi-day all-day events**: Represent a vacation or conference with one note, choose its first and last days in the form, and move or resize its calendar bar with undo across calendar views. File-menu editing and previews retain its inclusive range. ICS import/export, URL subscriptions and CalDAV preserve the whole span; existing single-day notes need no migration. See [All-day spans](./configuration/properties.md#all-day-spans-exclusive-storage-inclusive-display).
