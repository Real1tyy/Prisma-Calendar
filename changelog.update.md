### Fixed
- **Daylight saving no longer moves your event times**: on the morning a device switches to summer time, an hour of the local clock does not exist there — and a time that landed inside it used to be nudged an hour forward on that device only. A recurring instance, an end time Prisma derives from a start plus the default duration, and the **Sort Date** computed from either are now written with the same digits on every device, whichever of them is switching. All-day events keep their date even in zones whose clocks change at midnight. A recurring instance is also marked done once its own time has passed on the device you are looking at, matching the periodic scan and the rest of Prisma's past-event handling.
