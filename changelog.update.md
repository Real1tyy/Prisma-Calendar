### Added

- **Operation history**: a new toolbar button (and the hotkey-able **Show operation history** command) opens a scrollable log of your recent actions for the current planning system — what happened, which event it affected, when, and whether it's currently applied or undone. Renames name both the old and new event title. Undo or redo *any single row* without disturbing the others, or use **Revert to here** / **Restore to here** to roll a whole span back or forward; sort newest- or oldest-first. Undoing something no longer throws away the ability to redo it later, and the history now keeps your last 100 actions (up from 50). The log is saved on your device so it's still there — timestamps and all — after a restart; rows from an earlier session are shown as a record and marked **Previous session**, because replaying a days-old change would overwrite whatever you've done since. Each vault keeps its own log, and deleting a planning system discards its log along with it. See [Undo/Redo → Operation History](./features/management/undo-redo.md#operation-history).
