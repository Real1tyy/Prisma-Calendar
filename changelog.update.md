### Improved

- I added an overlapping event width setting, with 50%, 60%, two-thirds, 70%, three-quarters, and 80% options so you can give overlapping event text more room.

### Fixed

- Turning off **Allow event overlap** places overlapping timed events side-by-side and keeps them draggable and resizable onto occupied times, instead of locking them in place.
- The "+N more" link in week and day views uses your theme's colours instead of a light grey box with near-white text that was unreadable in dark themes.
