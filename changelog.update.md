### Improved

- Overlapping timed events in week and day views now sit side by side in their own columns by default, and I switched every existing calendar to this layout once. Turn **Allow slot event overlap** back on for a Google Calendar–style cascade, where each covered event's title wraps into the part that stays visible instead of running under the event in front. With the cascade on, the new **Overlapping event width** setting (50%, 60%, two-thirds, 70%, three-quarters or 80%) sets how wide the front-most event is.

### Fixed

- **Allow event overlap** and **Allow slot event overlap** describe what they actually do: the first decides whether you can drop or resize an event onto an occupied time, the second how overlapping events are drawn. Both apply to an open calendar straight away.
- The "+N more" link in week and day views uses your theme's colours instead of a light grey box with near-white text that was unreadable in dark themes.
