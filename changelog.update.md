### Added

- New **Doctor** page — open it from the command palette or the General settings page to see whether indexing, settings and your license are healthy, each with a plain-language explanation and a one-click fix where one exists. **Create bug report** carries the same findings straight into the report form, **Copy debug** puts a ready-to-paste bundle of the last 15 minutes of activity, every recent warning and error, and your version and platform on the clipboard, and **Save debug to file** writes it beside your calendar exports — folder and note names are abbreviated and secrets are never included.

### Improved

- Bug reports attach the Doctor's findings as well as your logs, as two separate choices you can review and untick independently: **Include a health check** (what the plugin thinks is wrong) and **Include recent logs** (the evidence behind it). Both are on by default for a bug report, off for anything else, and both are redacted before they leave your device.
