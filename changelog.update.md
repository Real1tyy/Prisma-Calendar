### Added

- **Event time display modes**: Choose **Normal**, **Small**, **Hidden**, or **Auto** separately for timed events in week/day and month/year views. Auto responds to the rendered tile: time drops from short or narrow tiles and returns as the tile has room, while phone month views keep their compact title-only layout. Configure both under **Settings → Calendar → Event appearance**. See [Calendar Settings](./configuration/calendar-ui.md#visual-appearance).
